# Refund & Return Policy

## 1. Return Window
Customers can request a return and full refund within **30 days of the purchase date**. Items requested after 30 days are ineligible for refunds or store credits.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements
To qualify for a refund, returned items must meet all of the following conditions:
- The item must be in its original, undamaged packaging.
- The item must include all original accessories, manuals, and warranty cards.
- A valid order number or proof of purchase must accompany the request.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items
The following products are strictly non-refundable:
- Digital gift cards and voucher codes.
- Downloadable software licenses and digital media once keys are issued.
- Opened consumable items and health/sanitary supplies.

## 4. Refund Processing Times
- Once our warehouse receives and inspects the returned item, return approval takes **1 to 2 business days**.
- Approved refunds take **5 to 7 business days** to process and reflect on the customer's original payment method.
- Shipping fees paid on the original order are non-refundable unless the return is due to a shipping error on our part.
