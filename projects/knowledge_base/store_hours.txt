Store Hours and Operating Schedule

Retail Storefront Operating Hours:
- Monday through Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST
- Saturday: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST
- Sunday: Closed all day

Customer Support Contact Center Hours:
- Phone & Live Chat Support: Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST
- Weekend Phone Support: Saturday from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM EST
- Ticket submission via web portal: Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

Holiday Schedule:
The storefront and phone support lines are completely closed on the following official holidays:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (First Monday in September)
- Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
- Christmas Day (December 25)

On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, retail storefront operations close early at 2:00 PM EST.
