# Shipping Policy & Delivery FAQ

## 1. Shipping Options and Timelines
We offer three domestic shipping tiers across the United States:
- **Standard Shipping:** Delivers within **3 to 5 business days**. Costs a flat rate of **$4.99**, or **Free** for all orders totaling $50 or more before taxes.
- **Express Shipping:** Delivers within **1 to 2 business days**. Costs a flat rate of **$14.99**.
- **Overnight Shipping:** Delivers on the **next business day by 3:00 PM local time**. Costs a flat rate of **$29.99**. Orders must be placed before **1:00 PM EST** Monday through Friday to qualify for same-day dispatch.

## 2. Free Shipping Threshold
- Free standard shipping automatically applies to carts with a subtotal of **$50.00 or greater** after all discounts or promotional codes are applied.
- Gift cards and subscription renewals do not count toward the $50 free shipping minimum.

## 3. Shipping Coverage and Restrictions
- We currently ship to all 50 US states, Washington D.C., and APO/FPO military addresses.
- Deliveries to Alaska, Hawaii, and military addresses may require 2 to 4 additional business days beyond the standard estimate.
- We do not currently support international delivery outside the United States.

## 4. Carrier Partners and Tracking
- Standard packages are handled by USPS (United States Postal Service).
- Express and Overnight packages are handled by FedEx Priority and UPS Air.
- A tracking number is automatically generated and emailed within 24 hours of package dispatch.
